# Journal rules extracted from the three Ministry circulars

Source: Iraqi Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, Research & Development Department.

- **14838/4 (23 Dec 2025)**: "Controls for establishing and issuing reputable peer-reviewed scientific journals" (the main checklist, 29 rules).
- **9420/1410 (26 Jan 2026)**: 11 recommendations from the first conference of Iraqi journal editorial boards.
- **11110/4 (6 Oct 2025)**: Procedure for establishing a journal or re-forming its editorial board (5 steps), plus "deans may not chair editorial boards".

## A. General controls (14838, Part 1)
1. Issuer is a government body, scientific institution, legally established study centre, or scientific society.
2. Issuer commits to funding the journal's continuity (official letter).
3. Papers are indexed and uploaded on the Iraqi Academic Scientific Journals (IASJ) site.
4. Journals indexed in Scopus / Clarivate are accepted, subject to R&D Department changes.
5. Has an electronic and/or print ISSN, registered under Iraq.
6. Has a legal deposit number from the National Library and Archives (Baghdad), shown on the cover.
7. Audited electronically through the journal website; first issue submitted as print + PDF.
8. Electronic-only publishing is allowed.
9. Single specialty only (e.g. computer science, Arabic language, civil engineering).
10. Max 60 papers per year before being indexed in Scopus or Clarivate.
11. At least one issue per year.
12. Editorial board members' papers are at most 10% of the papers in any single issue.
13. Journal name fits its general/specific specialty and is unique.
14. Uses an approved plagiarism-checking tool.
15. Has its own paper template on the website, showing journal and publisher logos and the citation style (IEEE, APA, ...).
16. Arabic-language journals: author names, affiliations, keywords, title and abstract translated into English.
17. Clear, detailed publication policy committing to research ethics and COPE.
18. Transparent, specialised peer-review process.
19. Publisher and management follow all R&D Department circulars, including approval of the editor-in-chief, managing editor and board members.

## B. Editorial board controls (14838, Part 2)
1. At least 20 members in the journal's specialty; 75% from outside Iraq and 25% from inside; members from different countries and continents (max 10% from any one foreign country).
2. Each member's Scopus h-index is at least 5 (sciences) or 2 (humanities); humanities may use international professors if this is not possible.
3. Editor-in-chief and managing editor h-index at least 6 (sciences) or 2 (humanities); otherwise a faculty member with a distinguished reputation and the highest output.
4. Members hold the title Lecturer or higher.
5. Editor-in-chief and managing editor hold Assistant Professor or higher.
6. Preferably 30% to 40% of members are Lecturers or Assistant Professors.

## C. Website (14838, Part 3)
1. Website in both Arabic and English.
2. Independent website, separate from the R&D Department's journals site.
3. Journal has DOI.
4. Website has: aims and scope, editorial board, publication ethics, author guidelines, reviewer guidelines, indexing databases, contact, archiving, publication fees (and any discounts).

## D. Recommendations (9420)
1. No combining an administrative post (university president, dean, head of department) with editor-in-chief or managing editor.
2. Editor-in-chief and board chosen on clear criteria (title, experience, citations, competence).
3. Minimum h-index for editor-in-chief; more international members.
4. Board members' papers at most 10% per issue; no coercive self-citation.
5. Journals unit in each college (admin).
6. 3 to 5 year plan to enter Scopus / Clarivate, with a follow-up committee.
7. Annual award for best journal (admin).
8. Partnerships with reputable universities and journals to attract international authors and reviewers.
9. Board members from various local and international universities.
10. Use modern tools including AI services in publishing.
11. Specialised journals across universities, avoiding duplication.
